Implementation & customisation

Implementation project — included in your package

Our professional services team handles deployment, configuration, and integration into your process. Scope by tier:

  • › S (Pilot): 10 man-days — basic setup, single department rollout
  • › M (Standard): 20 man-days — full configuration, multi-team integration, go-live
  • › L (Enterprise): 50 man-days — complex rollout, multiple departments, SSO, custom workflows

Custom development — CAPEX or OPEX

Beyond standard implementation, you choose the commercial model:

  • ›CAPEX: One-off project, fixed scope, separate proposal. Your annual package price unchanged.
  • ›OPEX: Roll the scope into your annual package. One total price, no separate investment.

Not included (unless contracted separately)

We're transparent about scope. These items require a separate agreement:

  • —Migration from legacy systems to ClaimSense
  • —Deployment to your own infrastructure (e.g., private cloud, on-premises)
  • —Special network or security architecture
  • —Regulatory customisation outside the product roadmap
